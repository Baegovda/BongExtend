Checker Plus for Gmail source included in BongExtend

Upstream: https://jasonsavard.com/?ref=homepage_url&ext=gmail

The original popup, options page, background code, account helpers, locales,
and assets are included from the installed upstream package. BongExtend keeps
one extension manifest and service worker; page and import paths are adjusted
to let the two projects share them. The original OAuth client configuration
is retained in the merged manifest.
