Chrono Download Manager source included in BongExtend

Upstream: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/chrono-download-manager/mciiogijehkdemklbdcbfkefimifhecn
Integrated package version: 0.13.12

The original manager, settings page, service worker, page helpers, locales, and
assets are included from the installed upstream package. The service worker
copy uses a fixed `globalThis` function for the WASM global lookup instead of
runtime JavaScript generation, and renames Chrono's private expression method
without changing its logic. BongExtend's root manifest remains authoritative
so both projects share one extension identity and one MV3 service worker.
Third-party notices shipped with Chrono are kept in their original locations,
including libs/LICENSE.txt.
